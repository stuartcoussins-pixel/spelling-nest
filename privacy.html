Privacy notice

Spelling Nest beta · last updated 17 September 2026

For children

Spelling Nest remembers your first name or nickname, your school year, your spelling words and how well you are doing, so Kit the owl can help you practise. Only your grown up can see this. We do not show adverts and we do not share it with anyone else.

Who we are

Spelling Nest is a free beta spelling practice app run by Stuart Coussins. You can contact us at info@spellingnest.co.uk about anything in this notice.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we need it
Parent or carer email addressTo send your sign in link and keep your family's data in one account
Date and time you agreed to this noticeTo record your consent
A 4 digit grown up PIN, stored only in scrambled (hashed) formTo stop children changing word lists and settings
Each child's first name or nickname and, if given, school yearTo greet each child and keep their progress separate
Word lists you addTo build spelling lessons
Lesson answers summarised as XP, streaks, badges, mastered words and lesson scoresTo show progress and choose which words to practise
A random identifier stored in your browserTo understand how the app is used, without identifying anyone
Usage events: which parts of the app are used and how long lessons takeTo see what is working and fix what is not. These never include spelling words, children's names or the content of messages you send us

We do not collect children's full names, dates of birth, photos, location or contact details. The app has no adverts and no third party tracking. Usage events are stored in our own database and reported only as totals and averages. Words are read aloud by your own device's built in voice, so they are not sent to us.

Our lawful basis

We rely on your consent as the parent or carer, given when you first sign in. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your family's data in Grown ups.

Where your data is kept

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in its London (United Kingdom) data centre region. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages at spellingnest.co.uk. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the app's sign in code is loaded from the jsDelivr network. Access to your family's data is restricted so that only your signed in account can read or change it.

Two other services handle email for us:

  • Resend sends your sign in links, using Amazon Web Services in Ireland. Your email address and the link are processed there.
  • Zoho Mail holds our info@spellingnest.co.uk mailbox on its European Union servers, so anything you email us is stored there.

Some of these providers are based outside the United Kingdom. Where data leaves the United Kingdom, they act under contract terms intended to keep your data protected to United Kingdom standards.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use Spelling Nest. When the beta ends, or if your account is not used for 12 months, we will delete it. You can delete everything yourself at any time.

Your rights

  • See, correct or delete your family's information. Most of this you can do yourself in Grown ups, including "Delete all our family's data".
  • Ask us to remove your email address from the sign in system, by emailing info@spellingnest.co.uk.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.
